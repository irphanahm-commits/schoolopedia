## 3. Comprehensive 5-Subject Curriculum Taxonomy & Progression

Every jurisdiction in Schoolopedia is mapped across 5 core discipline pillars:

### 3.1 Mathematics
* **Elementary (Grades 1–5 / Primary)**:
  * Number Sense, Place Value, Multi-Digit Addition & Subtraction
  * Multiplication, Division, and Fact Families
  * Fractions Foundations (Parts of a Whole, Equivalence, Addition with Like Denominators)
  * Decimal Place Value & Metric/Customary Measurement
  * 2D & 3D Geometric Shapes, Angles, and Perimeter/Area
* **Middle School (Grades 6–8 / Junior Secondary / KS3)**:
  * Ratios, Rates, Percentages, and Proportional Relationships (`6.RP` / `7.RP`)
  * Rational Numbers, Integers, and Absolute Value (`6.NS` / `7.NS`)
  * Algebraic Expressions, Distributive Property, and One-Step Equations (`6.EE` / `7.EE`)
  * Multi-Step Linear Equations with Variables on Both Sides (`8.EE.C.7` — Core Anchor)
  * Linear Functions, Slope-Intercept Form ($y = mx + b$), and Bivariate Data
  * Pythagorean Theorem, Volume of Cylinders, Cones, and Spheres (`8.G`)
* **High School (Grades 9–12 / Senior Secondary / GCSE / A-Levels / AP)**:
  * **Algebra 1 / Integrated Math I**: Quadratic Equations (Factoring, Completing Square, Quadratic Formula), Systems of Linear Equations & Inequalities, Exponential Functions.
  * **Geometry / Integrated Math II**: Formal Geometric Proofs, Triangle Congruence & Similarity, Right-Triangle Trigonometry ($\sin, \cos, \tan$), Circles and Coordinate Geometry.
  * **Algebra 2 / Integrated Math III**: Polynomial Functions, Rational & Radical Equations, Logarithmic Functions, Complex Numbers.
  * **Pre-Calculus & Trigonometry**: Unit Circle Trigonometry, Trigonometric Identities, Vectors, Matrices, Conic Sections, Limits.
  * **Calculus (AP / A-Level)**: Differential Calculus (Derivatives, Product/Quotient/Chain Rule, Optimization), Integral Calculus (Riemann Sums, FTC, U-Substitution), Differential Equations.
  * **Probability & Statistics**: Normal Distributions, Confidence Intervals, Hypothesis Testing, Linear Regression.

### 3.2 Science
* **Elementary (Grades 1–5 / Primary)**:
  * Earth Systems (Weather, Rock Cycle, Water Cycle)
  * Life Sciences (Plant & Animal Structures, Habitats, Life Cycles)
  * Physical Sciences (States of Matter, Simple Machines, Light, Heat, Sound)
* **Middle School (Grades 6–8 / Junior Secondary / KS3)**:
  * Structure, Function & Information Processing in Living Organisms (`MS-LS1`)
  * Photosynthesis & Cellular Respiration Cycles (`MS-LS1-6`)
  * Forces, Motion & Newton's Laws of Motion (`MS-PS2-1`)
  * Earth's Atmosphere, Weather Patterns & Plate Tectonics (`MS-ESS2`)
* **High School (Grades 9–12 / Senior Secondary / GCSE / A-Levels / AP)**:
  * **Biology**: DNA Structure, Gene Expression, Mendelian & Molecular Genetics, Cellular Organelles, Natural Selection, Ecological Energy Flow (`HS-LS1`, `HS-LS3`).
  * **Chemistry**: Atomic Theory, Electron Configurations, Periodic Trends, Chemical Bonding (Covalent, Ionic, Metallic), Stoichiometry, Gas Laws, Acid-Base Reactions, Thermodynamics (`HS-PS1`).
  * **Physics**: Kinematics (1D and 2D Motion), Newton’s Three Laws of Motion, Work, Energy and Conservation of Momentum, Gravitation, Waves, Optics, Electricity & Magnetism (`HS-PS2`, `HS-PS3`).
  * **Earth & Space Science**: Plate Tectonics, Stellar Evolution, Geochemical Cycles, Climate Change Science.

### 3.3 English Language Arts (ELA) / English Literature
* **Elementary (Grades 1–5 / Primary)**:
  * Phonics, Word Recognition, Fluency, and Foundational Spelling
  * Reading Comprehension: Main Idea, Supporting Details, Story Elements
  * Narrative and Informative Paragraph Construction, Grammar & Punctuation
* **Middle School (Grades 6–8 / Junior Secondary / KS3)**:
  * Analyzing Central Ideas, Inferences, and Textual Evidence in Informational Texts (`RL.8.1` / `RI.8.1`)
  * Author's Point of View, Tone, Metaphors, and Figurative Language
  * Argumentative Writing: Claims, Reasons, Textual Evidence, and Counterclaims (`W.8.1`)
  * Research Synthesis: Evaluating Credible Sources and Avoiding Plagiarism
* **High School (Grades 9–12 / Senior Secondary / GCSE / A-Levels / AP)**:
  * Rhetorical Analysis of Seminal Historical Documents (Logos, Pathos, Ethos, Rhetorical Fallacies)
  * World Literature, Classical Drama (Shakespeare), and Comparative Literary Analysis
  * Advanced Expository & Research Essays with MLA/APA Citations
  * Public Speaking, Debate, and Oral Rhetorical Communication

### 3.4 Social Studies, History & Civics
* **Elementary (Grades 1–5 / Primary)**:
  * Local Communities, Maps, Geography, and Citizen Roles
  * Foundations of State and National Symbols and History
* **Middle School (Grades 6–8 / Junior Secondary / KS3)**:
  * Democratic Principles: The US Constitution, Separation of Powers, and Bill of Rights (`C3.CIV.1`)
  * Ancient & Classical Civilizations (Mesopotamia, Egypt, Greece, Rome, Indus Valley)
  * Medieval & Early Modern World Civilizations
  * Geography: Human Geography, Demographics, and Resource Distribution
* **High School (Grades 9–12 / Senior Secondary / GCSE / A-Levels / AP)**:
  * Modern US / British / Commonwealth History (Revolutionary Periods, World Wars, Civil Rights Era)
  * Government & Civics: Legislative, Executive, Judicial Powers, Federalism, and Supreme Court Case Law
  * Macroeconomics & Microeconomics: Supply and Demand, Fiscal & Monetary Policy, Global Trade
  * International Relations & Modern World History

### 3.5 Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence
* **Elementary (Grades 1–5 / Primary)**:
  * Digital Citizenship & Online Safety
  * Sequencing, Loops, and Basic Block-Based Coding (Scratch / Blockly)
* **Middle School (Grades 6–8 / Junior Secondary / KS3)**:
  * Computational Thinking & Algorithmic Problem Solving
  * Introduction to Text-Based Programming in Python: Variables, Conditionals, Loops (`CSTA.2-AP-10`)
  * Data Representation: Binary, Hexadecimal, and Digital Media Encoding
  * Hardware vs Software, Networks, and The Architecture of the Internet
* **High School (Grades 9–12 / Senior Secondary / GCSE / A-Levels / AP)**:
  * AP Computer Science Principles & GCSE Computer Science
  * Object-Oriented Programming (Python / Java / TypeScript): Classes, Objects, Inheritance
  * Data Structures: Lists, Dictionaries/Hash Maps, Stacks, Queues, Binary Trees
  * Web Technologies: Full-Stack Architecture, REST APIs, Relational Databases (SQL)
  * Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Foundations: Data Pipelines, Neural Networks, Bias & Ethics
